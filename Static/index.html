CoverFlat

Flatten STEP surfaces into cutting patterns, with seam allowance and DXF export.

0 turns automatic seam splitting off. Edges closer than this are treated as sewn together and get matching notches. 0 turns it off. Where a body bends sharply, a dashed fold line is drawn. 0 turns it off.
Download DXF Sewing instructions

Each body in the STEP file becomes a panel. Panels that stretch more than the limit are split automatically into smaller panels joined by a seam, with matching notches on both sides.

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Pick a colour for each body, then click Flatten. Colours you pick are used in the preview and the work instruction.
Selected: none
Drag a panel to move it. Moves, rotations and flips carry into the DXF and the work instruction. A panel can come out mirrored, so use Flip if edges will not match.
